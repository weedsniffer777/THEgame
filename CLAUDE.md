# THEgame: jet dogfighter (working title)

## Ground rules
- Three.js + Vite, plain JavaScript. Target: CrazyGames (Poki dropped: its audience wants something far simpler than a flight game); must run well on Chromebooks.
- Minimal text in the game. No labels, tooltips or explanatory copy; communicate visually. Allowed text so far: "CLICK TO START"; the flight HUD's numbers (speed tape and readout with KT, altitude tape and readout, G, heading tape and readout); the 1/2/3 weapon key hints; enemy distances (only on the nearest enemy, the one nearest the screen centre and the gun or missile target; nm, then ft inside 1.5 nm, or km/m); and enemy aircraft types in plain words ("Fighter", "Bomber"; never real names). Ticks, notches and numbers are fine; letters only where they are really needed. Menus (pause: RESUME, RESTART, SETTINGS, BACK TO MENU, each with an icon on its left (play in a circle, cycling arrows, gear, door with an arrow out); settings tabs CONTROLS / UI / GRAPHICS / AUDIO; CONTROLS: MOUSE / TOUCH SENSITIVITY and its value, KEYS with the action names (PITCH DOWN, FIRE, TRACK ENEMY...), group names, RESET, PRESS A KEY, TOUCH LAYOUT, EDIT LAYOUT, the editor's RESET / DONE; UI: UNITS, HUD COLOR; GRAPHICS: SCREEN SHAKE DEFAULT/LESS/OFF) use short uppercase words. Tutorial prompts (a short sentence-case line high in the centre, with big keycaps that fill green while held, optionally in labelled groups such as Pitch / Roll / Yaw, Speed up / Slow down; never "click to continue") and the mission-complete and death screens (white text straight over the blurred picture, no panel; the death screen is only RETRY for now). The ring round the reticle: enemy type and km by its pointer. Status notices a little below the centre (white, sentence case, each in its own fixed row): Airbrake on, Landing gear extended. Banners across the screen: Leaving mission area, (countdown) Outside mission area, Mission complete, Mission failed. Warnings high in the centre (near-white with a hint of red, a bit bigger, steady, one per row): Slow down to extend landing gear!, Out of missiles!, Out of rockets! (when the selected weapon runs dry it switches to guns; an empty weapon can't be selected). Touch buttons GEAR and BRAKE. A bright solid diamond on each missile; enemy missiles show their distance (nm, ft inside 1.5 nm), ours none. Dev-only tools (the ` panel) are exempt.
- No real aircraft names, manufacturers, countries, flags or insignia anywhere in the game or store pages. Repaint all models.
- Every third-party asset must be commercial-use licensed (CC0, CC-BY, or a store license; never NC or ND) and listed in CREDITS.md.
- No sound yet.

## Layout
- `src/config.js`: every tunable number (flight model, mouse-aim instructor, camera, world).
- Camera (`src/camera.js`): held right click turns the view to an enemy (missile seeker target, else gun target, else nearest; picked at the press and kept while held; if it goes down the view zooms out and it takes a new press) and zooms, without changing where the jet is steered.
- Screen shake (main.js): explosions near the camera (weapons.S.onBoom), accelerating through Mach 1 (not slowing back through it).
- Mission areas (`src/mission/area.js`): a polygon per mission (Mission 1: a band along the valley clipped to the map), red straight lines on the maps with the outside hatched; within 0.5 nm of the edge a 'Leaving mission area' banner, outside it a 20 s countdown, then mission failed (over the countdown the 'plane lost' look, red edges and drained colour, builds up from nothing; it clears in a moment back inside); an enemy getting out fails it too. Banners across the screen (solid pale glowing bands, on with one quick flicker; Rajdhani, the one place it's used, everything else keeps its font): Mission complete (green), Mission failed / area warnings (red).
- `src/flight/`: atmosphere, flight model (`aircraft.js`), mouse-aim autopilot (`instructor.js`), flight of doomed enemies from what is left of their airframe (`crippled.js`).
- `src/world/`: CDLOD terrain from a heightmap (mirrors past the map edge) and the shared sky/haze.
- `src/jet.js`: F-5 model, control surface pivots, gear/doors, afterburner. Surface travel and flap schedule come from the plane's config (`F5.surfaces`), never hard-coded.
- `src/damage.js`: break-apart damage model shared by every aircraft (parts, hit points, debris, flight effects); per-type region tables (REGIONS).
- `src/enemy.js`: enemy aircraft types (the early swept-wing jet, gear removed; the four-engine bomber with a bomb bay built onto its belly: setBombBay(object, 0..1)), split into parts.
- `src/weapons.js`: guns (tracer per round), missiles (6 stations, no reload; never an automatic kill: the warhead's blast (warheadBlast) damages each part by its distance and the airframe by the distance from its surface, ~centre distance minus 0.6 x hitRadius, falling off to 0 at WEAPONS.missileBlast; 220 at the burst kills on a hit by damage alone, rockets' warheads the same), rockets (weapon 3, only on a jet with pod stations: fired one at a time alternating pods while held, unguided: a short motor burn then gravity, burst on the ground or near an aircraft with blast damage (a big fireball and a thick dark cloud that lingers); a white motor core in a big yellow glow, a very short hot streak, then thick dark smoke puffed along the burn (S.rocketSmoke); each launch blasts flame and smoke out of the pod's back; S.rocketPip, the impact point, drawn as the HUD pipper), seeker/lock, explosions, target drone. `src/rocketPod.js`: our own B-13-style pod (5 rockets, built in code; detailed both ends: a pointed front fairing the five launch tubes push out through, with rocket noses in their mouths, the tube exits with the motors' nozzles behind) and the rocket. `src/heatHaze.js`: the shimmer behind our nozzles (its quad is laid out in the shader with a mirrored basis, so the material is double-sided).
- Player jet paints (`F5_PAINTS` in jet.js; default 'grey', switchable in the model viewer): textures made by `tools/paint/f5_paints.py` from the texel-to-airframe map of `tools/paint/posmap.py` (the atlas is shared by both sides, so numbers and lettering are runtime decal cards, never painted into it).
- `src/paint.js`: paint schemes (per type in enemy.js / jet.js: texture file -> repainted file in `public/models/jets/paints/`). Enemies currently use 'standard'; 'unmarked' (no stars) is ready.
- `src/save.js`: the one save file (tutorialDone, settings, ...). `src/mission/mission1.js`: Mission 1 (the player starts at the foot of the big valley; formation flying up the valley, completion; with the tutorial two stragglers lead the lessons, afterwards all ten fly in formation ~2.5 nm ahead up the valley (~4.5 min to the far edge of the area); after the tutorial's lessons the rest of that formation flies a rounded square inside the area (weapons.toLoop), each on its own (all but the tutorial's two lesson stragglers): a hit bomber may lurch briefly off course (PEEL), and one that sees a missile coming (1 nm if aimed at it, ~600 m otherwise) breaks into a short, sharp turn, mostly diving (EVADE); both head back to their place in the formation once the break is done; ending: the last kill (always burning from its fuselage and wing roots, laid by the ending itself) cuts straight to a close front three-quarter shot held at a fixed offset off its nose (narrow FOV), then a slow wide shot from high up, the picture blurs and the results come up). `src/ui/tutorial.js`: tutorial overlay (line + keycaps that fill and pulse, multi-hole spotlight; never freezes the game), mission-complete card; the steps live in `main.js` (tutorialStep): flying (mouse, keys, throttle, airbrake; the hidden formation is on rails 2.2 km ahead), then guns on the jinking first straggler, which simply appears ahead with the map (each lesson's straggler is placed where the player is when it starts, mission.place: ~2.6 km ahead for guns, ~4.3 km for missiles, flying their way within 50 deg of the valley's line, clear of the ground for 15 km; the main formation is pushed at least 8 km ahead first) (no tracking lesson: tracking and the map are left to find, or to on-screen cues later; from no weapon selected; Aim by pointing the nose in the circle! / Shoot by pressing, one step, its line and spotlight kept until the kill), then missiles on the second, moved beyond gun range (select, wait for lock, launch; the enemy and our missiles spotlit; at the launch every weapon is put away and none can be picked until it's down, or the missile misses and missiles are picked again). Through both lessons the view follows and zooms on the lesson's enemy until it's down (tutForced; the player's own tracking is off, and on touch the zoom button stays hidden for the whole tutorial); on touch the fire button is empty while no weapon is selected. On touch there are no keycaps: the spotlight goes darker and a bobbing arrow points at the control (steering: a box in the middle with chevrons streaming out to both sides; throttle: an arrow with chevrons up and down beside it, sliding it either way is enough; green when done), and the airbrake button only appears at its step. Going for the main formation instead (hitting or downing any of its bombers) skips the rest: everything unlocks and it's the last step. Default mode is Mission 1; `?mode=free` (dev kit level selector) is free flight.
- `src/controls.js`: key bindings (PC). Every keyboard / mouse-button action (flight keys, throttle, airbrake, gear, fire, track, weapons, next target, map, pause; two slots each, defaults plus the player's changes from the save) goes through it: `held(id)` for held actions, presses dispatched in main.js (actDown/actUp). Mouse buttons are codes 'Mouse0'..'Mouse4'; labels follow the player's keyboard layout. The tutorial's keycaps come from the bindings and redraw if they change (showKeys / onBindings). Esc always pauses.
- `src/ui/settingsPage.js`: the settings page (shared by the pause menu and the main menu to come): big tabs; sized to fit inside the panel's slanted edge at its narrowest; the open tab scrolls with our own bar on the left and fades out at an edge with more beyond it, with an arrow there that jumps to that end; CONTROLS starts with MOUSE SENSITIVITY (TOUCH SENSITIVITY on touch; x0.25..x4 on a log slider, settings.mouseSens) and lists the bindings (click a slot, press a key or mouse button; Esc cancels, Backspace clears; a key already in use swaps over; RESET), or on touch the layout. In the settings the pause panel widens to most of the screen (a strip of the game left on the right). `src/ui/touchLayout.js`: touch layout editor (full screen over `public/ui/touch-layout.jpg`, a phone shot of the game without the controls; the real controls dragged anywhere, kept in the save as screen fractions; RESET / DONE); the tutorial's arrows and the throttle chevrons follow wherever the controls are. Touch has a pause button (right of the minimap).
- `src/settings.js`: player settings (units; HUD colour DEFAULT or GREEN: green turns every HUD element of ours green, enemy red stays; on the map we and our missiles are white) and unit conversions; `src/ui/pauseMenu.js`: pause menu with settings tabs.
- `src/hudFlight.js`: flight HUD (speed/altitude tapes, heading, G, throttle scale, pitch marks). `src/hudRing.js`: ring round the reticle with pointers to what is outside it (nearest enemy now; friendlies and missile warnings later). `src/hudMap.js`: minimap (top left; past the map's edge it shows mirrored copies, as the terrain does). `src/hudArms.js`, `src/hudXray.js`: weapon panel (one column per weapon, key number on top, weapons nose up; rockets in two stacked rows) beside the X-ray damage view, bottom right. `src/touch.js`: touch controls (flight left: plain throttle (thin black bar, square grey block, detent notches on one side, the power % over it; on touch it replaces the HUD's throttle scale), airbrake, gear just above it when slow/low; weapons right: fire, zoom, tap a weapon column; with the gear down the steering turns gentle, blended in with the gear: bank held to ~25 deg (INSTRUCTOR.gentleMaxBank), the rudder working harder and on into bigger turns, sideslip pushing the jet round 5x harder (ac.sideBoost) so flat turns work, and the wings levelling themselves whenever the finger isn't moving the aim). Missile lock: nose-fixed seeker circle, a small lock circle that slides onto the target.
- Dev kit (top-right button): model viewer with every animation, door angles and part breaking (and every weapon of the missile set). A jet can hand the rest of the game its own CG, nozzles, missile stations, gun ports, missile model and ground contact points (jet.cg / nozzles / rails / muzzles / missileProto / contacts; weapons.js and ground.js fall back to the F-5's). A MiG-21 was tried and shelved (its conversion scripts are in `tools/mig21/`). The PLANE selector switches the player jet (reloads): F-5, or SU-25 (TEST).
- `src/su25.js`: the Su-25 (dev kit only; converted with `tools/model/glb2json.py`; ~12 MB, only fetched when flown or picked in the viewer). Gear: each leg folds forward 90 degrees about a hinge searched at load so its wheel comes to rest at a measured spot inside (main wheels turned flat in their bays: the lower leg twists about its own axis as it folds, the upper strut and braces only fold; the nose wheel under the cockpit floor); each main bay's three doors (picked by size) close flat, and the big forward nose door (closed in the model with the gear down) is cut out of the skin and opens only while the gear moves. Canopy: tilts over to the right (hinged on the right sill). Its surfaces are separate pieces of the mesh, picked out by place and hung on hinges along their front edges: ailerons, inner and outer flaps (F-5 schedule), elevators on fixed stabilisers, lower rudder, and the airbrakes (each wingtip pod's rear splits into an upper and a lower petal). Small pieces of a moving surface ride its hinge: each elevator's trim tab and tip fairing, the rod at the back of each airbrake petal. Damage parts (REGIONS.su25; along each damage line a narrow band of the skin is cut into small cells that the split hands to one side or the other with a jitter, so breaks tear rather than slice; pylons, pod tails and other small pieces stay whole on their part; the outer flaps are cut at the outer wing's line; no bulkheads: those are the F-5's only (REGIONS.lightFighter.bulkheads)); toughness 2.2 (more hits, and collisions / hard landings it survives scale with toughness in weapons.js / ground.js). Wing vapour, wingtip vortices (off the pods), collar and contrail come from per-jet points (jet.fx). no afterburner (its throttle simply ends at 100 %: no burner detent on the keys, the touch lever or the HUD scale), its own CG, nozzles, 4 missile stations and 4 rocket pod stations (both measured from the pylons: outer two a side missiles, inner two pods), gun ports, ground contacts; a stronger heat haze off its nozzles (jets without a burner). Numbers `SU25` / `SU25_WEAPONS` (config.js): heavy, subsonic, a slower harder-hitting twin 30 mm with redder tracers (WEAPONS.tracer colours per plane). Weapon panel: a third column of 20 small rockets in two stacked rows of ten.
- `tools/terrain/`: swisstopo download and build scripts. Raw tiles live in `terrain-raw/` (git-ignored); built files in `public/terrain/<area>/`.

## Run
npm install, then npm run dev. Press ` in game for the tuning panel.
